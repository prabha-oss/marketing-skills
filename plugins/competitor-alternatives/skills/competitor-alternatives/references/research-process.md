# Competitor Research Process

## Deep Competitor Research

For each competitor, gather:

### 1. Product Research
- Sign up for free trial/demo
- Document features and UX
- Note limitations and pain points
- Screenshot key interfaces

### 2. Pricing Research
- Current pricing (all tiers)
- What's included at each level
- Hidden costs (setup, support, integrations)
- Contract terms and commitments

### 3. Review Mining
Sources:
- G2
- Capterra
- TrustRadius
- Reddit
- Twitter/X

Look for:
- Common praise themes
- Recurring complaints
- Specific use cases mentioned
- Switching reasons

### 4. Customer Feedback
- Talk to customers who switched TO you
- Talk to customers who switched FROM you
- Note objections during sales calls
- Survey churned customers

### 5. Content Research
- Their positioning and messaging
- Their own comparison pages
- Changelog and product updates
- Press releases and announcements

## Ongoing Updates

| Frequency | Task |
|-----------|------|
| Quarterly | Verify pricing, check for major feature changes |
| When notified | Customer mentions competitor change |
| Annually | Full refresh of all competitor data |
