# Length Limits (Enforced)

## Hero Section

| Element | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| Headline | Max 90 characters (prefer shorter) |
| Subheadline | Max 20 words, 1 sentence |
| Credibility/objection line | Max 12 words |
| CTA button | 2–4 words |
| CTA microcopy | Max 8 words |

## Features Section

| Element | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| Feature header | Max 6 words |
| Feature paragraph | Max 2 sentences, max 28 words total |

## Process Section

| Element | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| Step title | Max 6 words |
| Step subtitle | Max 14 words, 1 sentence |

## FAQ Section

| Element | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| FAQ answer | 2–3 sentences, max 60 words |

## General Guidelines

- Shorter is better
- One idea per element
- Concrete > vague
- Specific > general

## Checking Your Work

Before presenting options:
1. Count characters for headlines
2. Count words for paragraphs
3. Ensure single-sentence where required
4. Verify no run-on sentences
