# Banned Words & Phrases

## Do Not Use (Unless User Said Verbatim in Intake)

- transformation
- enablement
- partner / partnering
- revolutionary
- world-class
- cutting-edge
- leverage / leveraging
- seamless / seamlessly
- next-gen / next-generation
- solutions
- systems
- measurable ROI (unless proof exists)
- game-changer
- best-in-class
- industry-leading
- synergy
- empower / empowering
- unlock (as in "unlock your potential")
- journey (as in "customer journey")
- holistic
- robust
- scalable (unless specific)
- innovative (unless specific)
- disruptive
- paradigm
- ecosystem
- optimize (overused)
- streamline (overused)

## Replace With

| Instead of | Use |
|------------|-----|
| "solutions" | the actual deliverable name |
| "leverage" | "use" or "with" |
| "seamless" | describe what actually happens |
| "transform" | describe the before/after |
| "partner" | "work with" or "hire" |
| "robust" | describe the specific capability |
| "optimize" | describe what improves |

## Why These Are Banned

- They're vague
- Every competitor uses them
- They don't differentiate
- They don't create mental images
- Readers skip over them

## What to Use Instead

- Concrete deliverables
- Specific outcomes
- Real constraints
- Actual timelines
- Honest limitations
- Direct language

## Example Rewrites

```
BAD: "We provide seamless solutions to transform your business."
GOOD: "We build your sales playbook in 2 weeks. You get scripts, templates, and training."

BAD: "Our world-class team leverages cutting-edge technology."
GOOD: "4 senior engineers. Direct Slack access. 48-hour response time."

BAD: "Unlock your team's full potential with our innovative platform."
GOOD: "Your reps book 30% more meetings. Or we refund month one."
```
