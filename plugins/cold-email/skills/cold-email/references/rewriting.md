# Email Rewriting

## Process

1. **Cut to 50-120 words** — Remove all fluff
2. **One idea only** — Delete secondary points
3. **Remove filler phrases** — See list below
4. **Strengthen the CTA** — Make it softer, lower commitment
5. **Plain text only** — Remove formatting, images, HTML

## Phrases to Remove

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "I wanted to reach out because..."
- "I'm reaching out to..."
- "I thought I'd drop you a line"
- "I just wanted to..."
- "I'd love to..."
- "I was wondering if..."
- "Please let me know if..."
- Company history/background paragraphs
- Feature lists
- Multiple CTAs

## Before/After Example

### Before (187 words)

```
Hi {{Name}},

I hope this email finds you well. My name is John and I'm the Head of Sales at AcmeCorp. We're a leading provider of sales enablement solutions that help B2B companies increase their revenue.

I wanted to reach out because I noticed your company has been growing rapidly and I thought our solution might be a good fit. We offer a comprehensive platform that includes CRM integration, analytics, coaching tools, and automated workflows.

Our customers typically see a 40% increase in sales productivity and a 25% reduction in sales cycle length. We work with companies like BigCorp, MegaInc, and TechGiant.

I'd love to schedule a call to discuss how we can help your team achieve similar results. Would you be available for a 30-minute call next week? I'm free Tuesday at 2pm or Thursday at 10am.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
John
```

### After (52 words)

```
{{Name}},

Noticed {{company}} is scaling fast — usually means sales productivity becomes a bottleneck.

We've helped teams like BigCorp cut sales cycles by 25% without adding headcount.

Worth a quick chat to see if relevant?

John
```

## Rewriting Checklist

- [ ] Under 120 words?
- [ ] One clear pain point?
- [ ] One clear outcome?
- [ ] One soft CTA?
- [ ] No "I" in first sentence?
- [ ] No company intro paragraph?
- [ ] No feature lists?
- [ ] Plain text (no formatting)?
