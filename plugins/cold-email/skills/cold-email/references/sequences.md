# Follow-Up Sequences

## Sequence Structure

| Email | Timing | Purpose |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Email 1 | Day 0 | Initial outreach |
| Email 2 | Day 3 | Add value, different angle |
| Email 3 | Day 7 | Social proof or case study |
| Email 4 | Day 14 | Direct ask, new hook |
| Email 5 | Day 21 | Breakup email |

## Templates

### Email 2: Value-Add

```
Subject: Re: {{original subject}}

{{Name}},

Quick follow-up — thought this might be useful:

[One-sentence insight, stat, or resource relevant to their pain]

Still worth a chat?

{{Signature}}
```

### Email 3: Social Proof

```
Subject: Re: {{original subject}}

{{Name}},

Just wrapped a project with {{similar company}} — they were dealing with {{same pain}}.

Result: {{specific outcome}}

Happy to share what worked if helpful.

{{Signature}}
```

### Email 4: Direct Ask

```
Subject: Re: {{original subject}}

{{Name}},

I'll keep this short.

[New angle or hook — 1 sentence]

Worth 15 minutes this week?

{{Signature}}
```

### Email 5: Breakup

```
Subject: closing the loop

{{Name}},

Haven't heard back — guessing this isn't a priority right now.

If things change, happy to pick this up.

Closing the loop for now.

{{Signature}}
```

## Rules

- Never "just following up"
- Each email adds new value or angle
- Keep getting shorter
- Breakup email often gets highest reply rate

## Timing Variations

| Audience | Recommended Cadence |
|----------|---------------------|
| SMB | Days 0, 2, 5, 10, 17 |
| Mid-market | Days 0, 3, 7, 14, 21 |
| Enterprise | Days 0, 4, 10, 18, 28 |
