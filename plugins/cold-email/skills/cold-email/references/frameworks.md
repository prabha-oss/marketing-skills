# Email Frameworks

## Framework 1: PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)

**Best for:** Strong, known pain

```
Subject: {{pain}} at {{company}}?

{{Name}},

[State problem — 1 sentence]

[Agitate: cost of inaction — 1-2 sentences]

[Hint solution without pitching — 1 sentence]

[Soft CTA]

{{Signature}}
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: outbound at {{company}}?

{{Name}},

Most B2B teams I talk to are burning budget on outbound that doesn't convert.

The reps are dialing, but meetings aren't booking — and leadership is asking hard questions.

We've helped 30+ teams fix this without adding headcount.

Worth a quick chat?

{{Signature}}
```

---

## Framework 2: 4P (Personalization-Problem-Proof-Proposal)

**Best for:** Need credibility, cold audience

```
Subject: {{personalized hook}}

{{Name}},

[Personalization — show you did research — 1 sentence]

[Problem — what they likely struggle with — 1 sentence]

[Proof — specific result or credibility — 1 sentence]

[Proposal — soft CTA]

{{Signature}}
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: saw your Series B news

{{Name}},

Congrats on the raise — scaling the sales team is probably top of mind now.

Most post-Series B teams struggle to ramp new reps without killing unit economics.

We helped {{similar company}} cut ramp time by 40% — happy to share how.

Worth 15 min?

{{Signature}}
```

---

## Framework 3: AIDA (Modern Version)

**Best for:** General awareness, keep very short

```
Subject: {{attention-grabbing subject}}

{{Name}},

[Attention — hook relevant to them — 1 sentence]

[Interest — specific pain — 1 sentence]

[Desire — outcome — 1 sentence]

[Action — soft CTA]

{{Signature}}
```

---

## Framework 4: One-Line Email

**Best for:** Senior executives, C-suite

```
Subject: quick question

{{Name}},

Helping {{company type}} {{achieve outcome}} — worth a 10-min chat?

{{Signature}}
```

**Examples:**
```
Helping SaaS founders reduce churn by 30% — worth a quick call?
```

```
Helping agencies land enterprise clients without cold outreach — relevant?
```

```
Helping e-comm brands cut CAC while scaling — worth 10 min?
```

---

## Framework Selection Logic

| ICP Seniority | Pain Clarity | Best Framework |
|---------------|--------------|----------------|
| C-suite | Any | One-Line |
| VP/Director | Strong | PAS |
| VP/Director | Unclear | 4P |
| Manager | Strong | PAS |
| Manager | Unclear | AIDA |
